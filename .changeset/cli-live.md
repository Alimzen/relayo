Live keys and templates: `relayo tail` follows events as they happen (`--requests`: the API request log), `send` takes `--template` and `--var` and asks for `--from` with live keys, and `relayo templates list|show|pull|push` keeps templates as files in your repository. `RELAYO_URL` and `RELAYO_API_KEY` work besides `RELAYO_SIM_*`.
